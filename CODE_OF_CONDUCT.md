# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks, and
bad-faith disruption are not welcome.

## Expected

- Assume good intent; disagree on ideas, not people
- Keep feedback specific and actionable
- Respect maintainers' time and decisions on scope

## Unacceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, or threats
- Publishing others' private information
- Sustained derailing of discussions

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the repository maintainers (GitHub issues for public
matters; private contact or a security advisory for sensitive reports).
Maintainers may warn, edit/remove content, or block contributors.

This is a short project-specific policy inspired by the [Contributor
Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
